Tap Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tap Rush ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the App may process your chosen username, gameplay scores, leaderboard rankings, game preferences, and subscription status.

Advertising and platform service providers may also process device identifiers, diagnostic information, and information about your interactions with ads, subject to applicable law and privacy choices.

2. Advertising - Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to users who do not have an active ad-free subscription. Google and its partners may process information to deliver, measure, and protect advertisements.

Where required, the App requests advertising consent. You may be offered personalized or non-personalized advertising choices. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertisements.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Leaderboards - Supabase

The App uses Supabase to support online leaderboards. Your chosen username, game score, and ranking-related information may be stored and displayed to other players. Please avoid using sensitive personal information as your public username.

Learn more: Supabase Privacy Policy .

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App may offer subscriptions through Google Play. Google processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. The App may process purchase status and transaction identifiers to verify access to paid features and restore entitlements.

5. Local Storage

The App may store gameplay preferences, onboarding completion status, progress, and other settings locally on your device.

6. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process information to operate the game, provide leaderboards, maintain subscriptions, prevent abuse, and support advertising. Where applicable, processing relies on consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations. The relevant legal basis depends on the processing activity and applicable law.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers necessary for game functionality, including Google and Supabase. Public leaderboard information may be visible to other players.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may contact the developer to request access to or deletion of personal information where applicable.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. Advertising and data processing practices must comply with applicable age-related requirements. Additional protections may apply to younger users.

10. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where applicable.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices, or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy inquiries, data access, or deletion requests, contact the developer using the support contact information published on the App's Google Play listing.
